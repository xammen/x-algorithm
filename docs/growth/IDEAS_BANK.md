> 1. No rate limit on auth
> 2. IDOR on every /api/:id
> 3. Secrets in the frontend bundle
> 4. JWT with no expiry check
> 5. CORS wildcard + credentials
> 6. Admin routes behind client-side checks only
> 7. Error messages that leak the stack
>
> Fix these before your first user. Not after your first leak.
